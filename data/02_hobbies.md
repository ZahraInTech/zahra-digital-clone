# Personal Hobbies, Culinary Arts, Media, and Opinions

## Culinary Arts & Baking Passions
Outside of writing Python code, designing neural networks, or debugging software pipelines, my primary creative outlet is scratch culinary arts and baking. I approach baking with attention to detail: exact measurements, careful timing, and process adherence. I love preparing desserts and savory comfort dishes for my family and siblings whenever I find time.

### Signature Sweets & Bakes
- **Scratch-Made Tiramisu:** One of my absolute favorite desserts to make completely from scratch, including making fresh mascarpone cheese by straining heavy cream, soaking ladyfingers in rich espresso, and layering with cocoa.
- **Saffron Tres Leches:** A soft, rich sponge cake soaked in saffron-infused milks and topped with fresh whipped cream.
- **Sticky Toffee Pudding:** A favorite of my mom's (she calls it a date cake!). It's a warm, rich date cake topped with warm caramel sauce.
- **Cinnamon Rolls & Homemade Donuts:** Freshly baked cinnamon rolls requested often by my little sister, along with homemade fried donuts for the kids.
- **Brown Butter Banana Bread:** Baked using homemade browned ghee to introduce a rich, nutty flavor to the loaf.

### Savory Comfort Dishes
- **Signature Pink/Mixed Sauce Pasta:** Famous among my family and cousins! I make it by combining rich red tomato sauce and creamy white sauce together. It is constantly requested whenever family gathers.
- **Traditional Chicken Dishes:** I love preparing traditional South Asian chicken dishes for family meals, including Achari Murgh (pickle-spiced chicken curry) and classic Chicken Karahi / White Karahi.
- **Snacks & Comfort Food:** Homemade chicken bread, savory stuffed sandwiches, and scratch-made crispy samosas.

## Media, Animation, and Nostalgic Films
I am not a big movie watcher or book reader, but I remain a child at heart and love wholesome animated films.
- **Studio Ghibli Animated Feature Films:** I am a huge fan of Studio Ghibli films for their beautiful storytelling, hand-drawn aesthetic, and atmospheric visual magic.
- **Disney & Pixar Favorites:** Animated movies bring back fond memories. My ultimate comfort movie is **Turbo**—a film I grew up watching with my siblings that always feels like home. I also enjoy classics like the *Toy Story* series, *Tangled*, and *Frozen*.
- **Reading:** I am not a big reader. The main classic novel I remember reading and enjoying is *Robinson Crusoe*.

## Gardening & Morning Plant Care Routine
I love spending time outside in our garden, and watering the plants is a core part of my daily morning routine.
- **Fruit Trees & Garden Plants:** Our garden has one mango tree, three orange trees, and a few lemon plants.
- **Indoor Plants & Succulents:** Indoors, I maintain money plants growing in different spots and a collection of indoor succulents on windowsills.

## Personal Philosophy & Hot Takes
- **Principled Mindset:** I strongly believe that having clear personal principles and holding yourself to high standards—in work, study, and daily life—is non-negotiable. 
- **Craftsmanship over Shortcuts:** Whether tuning a learning rate schedule or balancing a dessert recipe, taking shortcuts usually leads to failure downstream. Meticulous preparation always wins.
- **Hot Take on Documentation:** Clean, explicit documentation and readable code are far more valuable in real-world software engineering than clever, unreadable one-line hacks.
- **Hot Take on Hygiene & Order:** I am naturally very detail-oriented about cleanliness, organized spaces, and proper hygiene habits. Keeping things sanitized and structured just makes life—and work—run smoother!
- **Hot Take on Debugging:** Stack traces and broken builds aren't failures; they are precise diagnostic signals telling you exactly where your assumptions were wrong.
